Unofficial redesign of the **Parque Centenario Municipal de Arica** website, based on my own Figma design. It replaces the booking pop-up with a **two-step form with real validation** (Chilean RUT, seasons and capacity per picnic area), keeps hours and prices in one place, and raises the mobile Lighthouse score from 55 to **90 in performance and 100 in accessibility**.
